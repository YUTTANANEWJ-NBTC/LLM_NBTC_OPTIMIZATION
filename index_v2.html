Rules of Thumb: When Each Strategy Earns Its Keep

Cheat sheet: pick by what you need — speed, capacity or cost — then measure on your own hardware!

Nscale Ray Summit
Strategy Reach for it when (ควรเลือกใช้) Think twice when (ต้องระวัง)
TP
Tensor 		Latency-sensitive serving inside one NVLink domain; dense layers shard cleanly. KV-hungry long context; links slower than NVLink; sizes that don't divide attention heads. Measure: KV capacity moved only 5.03M vs 5.05M tokens (TP8 vs DP8).
TEP ★
Tensor+Exp 		Sparse MoE like V4-Flash (6 of 256): each GPU owns whole intact experts, so they run at full speed with less cross-GPU traffic than pure TP. Dense models (nothing to shard); expert routing that would have to leave the NVLink domain; imbalanced experts without EPLB.
PP
Pipeline 		Crossing the backend network (InfiniBand or RoCE) between NVLink domains; freeing HBM once TP is maxed out. Low-batch, latency-critical traffic: pipeline bubbles dominate at small batch sizes.
DEP
Data+Exp 		Throughput-per-dollar at scale; the decode pool in P/D setups. Measure: on 8× B200, TEP8 beat DP8×EP (2,209 vs 1,634 tok/s)! Single-user latency (All-to-All bound); small setups where replicating attention wastes HBM.
DCP
Decode Ctx 		Decode-heavy, very long context: shards decode KV across the TP ranks. Prefill-heavy traffic; the DCP size must divide the TP size.

The Flags ARE the Architecture: vLLM & KubeRay RayService

Download the 167 GB weights once to shared RWX storage — switching strategies is just a single kubectl apply! 

# Strategy A (TEP8) — Pinned to vLLM v0.27.1
vllm serve /models/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 \
  --port 8000 \
  --distributed-executor-backend ray \
  --served-model-name deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 \
  --trust-remote-code \
  --kv-cache-dtype fp8 \
  --block-size 256 \
  --tensor-parallel-size 8 \
  --enable-expert-parallel \
  --moe-backend deep_gemm_mega_moe \
  --attention-config '{"use_fp4_indexer_cache":true}' \
  --tokenizer-mode deepseek_v4 \
  --max-model-len 262144
